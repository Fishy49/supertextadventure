# The Crystal Quest: fetch the Crystal of Power and bring it back to the wizard.
# Run it with: bin/world play games/the_crystal_quest.json --script games/the_crystal_quest.walkthrough.txt

talk to wizard
expect flag met_wizard
ask wizard about quest
expect flag quest_started
ask wizard about danger
expect says giant spider

open chest
take potion
take sword
expect has rusty_sword
expect has health_potion

north
take key
expect has rusty_key
north
expect room deep_forest

# Strike first. The spider attacks on its own after three actions in this room.
attack spider
attack
attack
expect flag spider_defeated
take silk
expect has spider_silk

use key on gate
expect flag gate_opened
expect not has rusty_key
north
expect room crystal_cave
take crystal
expect has power_crystal

south
south
south
expect room tavern
give crystal to wizard
expect flag quest_complete
expect has spell_book
expect not has power_crystal
